Where every number comes from.

Nothing on this site is estimated, rounded up for effect, or quoted second-hand. Each figure is below with its source and its data year, including the ones that weaken the case.

How to read the county figures

The county health numbers come from CDC PLACES, which produces model-based small-area estimates. They are not counts of real diagnoses in a county register. CDC fits a statistical model to Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System survey responses plus census data, then predicts a rate for every county in the country. That is the only way to get comparable county-level chronic-disease rates, and it is what the CDC itself publishes — but it means every county figure carries a confidence interval, and small differences between two counties should not be treated as meaningful.

The differences quoted here are not small ones. Somerset County's modelled stroke prevalence is two-thirds higher than Montgomery County's, and the Lower Shore counties sit above the state median on every measure at once, which is harder to explain away as model noise than any single number would be.

One comparison on the home page crosses sources: Somerset's 4.0% is a CDC PLACES county estimate, while Arkansas's 4.8% is a state-level figure from the American Heart Association's 2025 update. Both ultimately rest on BRFSS data and both are age-adjusted, but they are produced by different methods, so treat that as an illustration of scale rather than a like-for-like ranking.

What this project does not claim

ReMoRehab has not been tested in a trial. No outcome on this site is a result we produced. The claim being made is narrower than "this works": it is that mirror therapy has moderate-quality evidence behind it, that doing more of it is limited mainly by access rather than by complexity, and that access is measurably worse on Maryland's Lower Eastern Shore than almost anywhere else in the state. Whether a printed headset and a browser app meaningfully change outcomes is an open question that would need a study to answer.

Reproducing the county figures

The PLACES query used for the table on the home page is a single public API call, with no key required:

https://data.cdc.gov/resource/swc5-untb.json?stateabbr=MD&datavaluetypeid=AgeAdjPrv&$limit=5000

Filter the result to measureid values STROKE, DISABILITY, MOBILITY and ACCESS2, and group by locationname.
